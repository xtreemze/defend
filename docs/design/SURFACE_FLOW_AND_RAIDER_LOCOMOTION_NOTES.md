# Surface Flow and Raider Locomotion — Open Decisions

These are not yet production balance decisions.

- Minimum practical drainage gap relative to R1 diameter.
- Whether fluid can climb very small curbs or only follow continuously connected low surfaces.
- How long locally blocked energy should search before visibly pooling.
- Whether pooled energy pressure can eventually overflow a low obstacle.
- Whether tower destruction releases energy previously trapped beneath/around the foundation.
- Whether the silo exposes a stronger collection field at low reserve as an emergency behavior.
- R1 route-replanning cadence and acceptable CPU budget.
- R2 obstacle-cost model: expected impact time, expected structural damage, or simpler heuristic.
- R3 steering frequency and how much it should deliberately compensate for slope.
- Whether R2/R3 can physically damage T1 barriers through collision alone and at what rate.
- How surface deformation from eruptions updates both flow and raider navigation topology.
- How much drainage information the placement preview should expose without solving the layout for the player.
- Whether visible energy streams should intentionally reveal exploitable approach corridors to the raider player/AI.
