# Open questions for energy flow and world ecology

This note accompanies `ENERGY_FLOW_AND_WORLD_ECOLOGY.md` and exists to keep unresolved mechanics explicit rather than silently canonicalizing them.

## Needs experiments before production balance

- Collection delay from impact to silo credit.
- Whether energy ejected off-map is entirely lost or partially recoverable.
- Whether an active mothership siphon may capture loose surface energy.
- Whether defenders keep firing throughout extraction/evacuation or transition into a resolution state after breach.
- Raider return economics: reuse, repair, dissolve to energy, or refabricate.
- Silo-to-mothership and mothership-to-silo conversion costs.
- Travel cost model between teal signatures.
- Brightness mapping from stored energy, recent throughput, raid frequency, and defensive condition.
- How much uncertainty target brightness should retain.
- Whether settling creates a new fortress or restores/returns to a prior one.
- How long world targets evolve while the player is engaged elsewhere.

## Strong current direction

- Energy ownership changes should be physically visible.
- Defensive recovery is delayed until liberated energy reaches the silo.
- Siphoning reverses the same teal flow upward toward the mothership.
- Surviving raiders physically evacuate through that same stream.
- Strategic target selection uses distant teal signatures rather than conventional mission-node UI.
- Bright signatures generally mean rich, active, well-defended targets; dim signatures generally mean poor, quiet, deteriorated, easier targets.
- Target difficulty should emerge from economic history and physical defenses rather than arbitrary hidden stat multipliers.
- Economic value is authoritative and conserved independently from the number of rendered particles.
